# PHASE 5.3 PRE-EXECUTION DECISION RULES

**Date:** 2026-08-14

This document formally freezes the exact evaluation thresholds required to declare an outcome of `RETAIN`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, or `REMOVE` for any evaluated CVD Model (e.g., MODEL G) against its corresponding BASE control.

These thresholds are frozen *before* ML execution to prevent post-hoc hypothesis adjustment.

## 1. Aggregate Metric Calculation
The primary determination of overall improvement shall be evaluated using:
1. **Pooled Aggregate:** A global set of OOS predictions merged across all 6 symbols (maintaining strict chronological symbol alignment).
2. **Equal-Weighted Secondary Aggregate:** The simple arithmetic mean of the metric across the 6 independent symbol results, serving as a robustness check against one highly active symbol dominating the pooled metric.

## 2. Brier Deterioration Threshold
"Does not materially deteriorate" is strictly quantified as:
- The aggregate Brier Score of `BASE+CVD` must not exceed `BASE_MATCHED` by more than **+0.0005**.
- Condition: `Brier(BASE+CVD) <= Brier(BASE_MATCHED) + 0.0005`.

## 3. Multi-Symbol Evidence
- To be considered a valid generalized feature, the augmentation must improve OOS LogLoss on a **minimum of 4 out of 6** symbols independently.
- If it improves 3 or fewer symbols, the result cannot be classified as an unconditional `RETAIN` (it may be `INCONCLUSIVE` or `REMOVE` depending on other metrics).

## 4. Fold Consistency
- The aggregate OOS improvement must be robust across time.
- The `BASE+CVD` LogLoss must be superior to `BASE_MATCHED` in **at least 2 out of the 3** strictly chronological Walk-Forward folds.
- If the entire aggregate improvement is generated by only 1 fold, the condition fails.

## 5. Cost Robustness
- Let $\Delta ENR(cost) = ENR(G, cost) - ENR(BASE\_MATCHED\_G, cost)$.
- Positive $\Delta ENR$ strictly favors the CVD-augmented model.
- The condition requires strict relative improvement at predefined tiers:
  - $\Delta ENR(0 \text{ bps}) > 0$
  - AND $\Delta ENR(5 \text{ bps}) > 0$
  - AND $\Delta ENR(10 \text{ bps}) > 0$
- *Note:* Equality ($= 0$) constitutes a **failure** of the condition.

## 6. Statistical Acceptance
- **Primary Aggregate Test:** HAC-corrected Diebold-Mariano Test (Max Lag = 59) performed on the pooled OOS sequence.
  - *Secondary Reporting:* Per-symbol DM statistics and p-values are required as mandatory diagnostics (do not apply BH to these descriptive per-symbol metrics if they are just diagnostics, BH applies strictly to the predefined hypothesis families below).

### 6.1 Multiple Comparison Policy
- The Benjamini-Hochberg False Discovery Rate (FDR) control shall be used at **$q = 0.05$**.
- Explicit Hypothesis Families:
  - **Family A:** `MODEL G` vs `BASE_MATCHED_G` evaluated across the 6 individual symbols ($N=6$ hypotheses).
  - **Family B:** Ablation Models (B, C, D, E, F) vs their respective `BASE_MATCHED_X` controls, evaluated across 6 individual symbols ($N = 5 \times 6 = 30$ hypotheses).

### 6.2 Frozen Block Bootstrap Implementation
- **Algorithm:** Deterministic moving-block bootstrap (contiguous chronological blocks).
- **Sampling:** With replacement.
- **Block Length:** Primary = 60 observations (target horizon). Sensitivity = 30 and 120 observations.
- **Constraints:**
  - Blocks must **never** cross symbol boundaries.
  - Each symbol's OOS sequence is resampled independently.
  - Final incomplete blocks (length < 60) are discarded to maintain exact temporal block weighting.
  - Paired `BASE` and `CVD` predictions/observations remain structurally locked together inside every resampled block.
- **Statistic:** Paired $\Delta LogLoss$ and $\Delta Brier$.

### 6.3 Frozen Block Permutation Implementation
- **Algorithm:** Contiguous block permutation.
- **Block Length:** 60 observations.
- **Iterations:** 1000 permutations.
- **Constraints:**
  - Permutation is performed independently within each symbol.
  - Blocks cannot cross symbol boundaries.
  - The `BASE` features/predictions remain completely fixed in their native chronological sequence.
  - Only the `CVD` augmentation blocks are temporally shuffled to destroy the relationship with the target while preserving localized CVD autocorrelation.
- **Master Seed:** 42 (with deterministic child seeds per iteration/symbol).
